##  What is GlitchPad?

**GlitchPad** is a custom gaming macropad built around the **Seeed XIAO RP2040**, **KMK firmware**, a rotary encoder, OLED display, and **20 SK6812 Mini-E RGB LEDs**.

It's designed mainly for FPS gaming, while still being fully customizable for other uses.

### Features

*  6 Cherry MX-compatible switches
*  EC11 rotary encoder
*  0.91" OLED display
*  20 × SK6812 Mini-E LEDs
*  XIAO RP2040
*  Custom 3D-printed case
*  Custom PCB
*  KMK firmware
*  Cyber-blue glitch lighting

---

##  Button Layout

```text
             SPACE       UP

             SHIFT      LEFT      DOWN      RIGHT
```

The rotary encoder is designed for quick number-based controls, such as switching between weapon slots in FPS games.

---

##  OLED + RGB

The OLED features a custom glitch-style animation, while the 20 SK6812 Mini-E LEDs provide a **cyber-blue pulse** aesthetic.
